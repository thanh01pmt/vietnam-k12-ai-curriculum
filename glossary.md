# Glossary: Vietnam K-12 AI & Informatics Curriculum Terminology

Alphabetical glossary of statutory, pedagogical, and technical terms established across Thông tư 32/2018, Thông tư 13/2022, Quyết định 2422/QĐ-BGDĐT, Công văn 5588/BGDĐT-GDPT, and related regulatory documents.

---

- **12 Tiết định mức tối thiểu (12-Period Mandatory Benchmark)** — The statutory minimum annual instructional duration dedicated to AI content across all K-12 grade levels starting in academic year 2026–2027 (Ch 7).
- **5 Phẩm chất chủ yếu (5 Core Qualities)** — The overarching national character qualities prescribed by GDPT 2018: Yêu nước (Patriotism), Nhân ái (Compassion), Chăm chỉ (Diligence), Trung thực (Integrity), Trách nhiệm (Responsibility) (Ch 1).
- **3 Năng lực chung (3 General Competencies)** — Core cross-curricular capabilities developed across all subjects in GDPT 2018: Tự chủ và tự học (Self-reliance), Giao tiếp và hợp tác (Communication), Giải quyết vấn đề và sáng tạo (Problem-solving) (Ch 1).
- **4 Mạch nội dung giáo dục AI (4 AI Curricular Threads)** — The four continuous knowledge pillars defined by QĐ 2422: Thread A (Concepts & Apps), Thread B (Data & ML), Thread C (Techniques & Systems), Thread D (Societal Impact & Ethics) (Ch 2).
- **5E Instructional Model (Mô hình 5E)** — An inquiry-based pedagogical sequence comprising Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, and Evaluate, strongly recommended for STEM and AI classroom lessons (Ch 6).
- **Ảo giác AI (AI Hallucination)** — The tendency of generative models to produce plausible-sounding but factually fabricated or ungrounded assertions (Ch 4, Ch 5).
- **Bản quyền số & Liêm chính học thuật (Digital Copyright & Academic Integrity)** — Normative ethical requirements prohibiting plagiarism, unauthorized data scraping, and unattributed AI-assisted school submissions (Ch 4, Ch 5).
- **Bảo vệ dữ liệu cá nhân (Personal Data Protection)** — Statutory privacy obligations codified under Nghị định 13/2023/NĐ-CP safeguarding student identities, biometrics, and browsing histories from unauthorized collection or commercialization (Ch 5, Ch 7).
- **Cảm biến (Sensor)** — A hardware transducer converting environmental physical properties (light, sound, pressure, distance) into electrical and digital signals for machine processing (Ch 3).
- **Chuyên đề học tập (Specialized Modules)** — 3 elective academic modules (35 periods/year total) in Grades 10–12 designed for in-depth scientific, technical, or applied exploration (Ch 1, Ch 5).
- **Công văn 5208/BGDĐT-GDPT (2026)** — MOET directive establishing core general education tasks and digital transformation guidelines for school year 2026–2027 (Ch 7).
- **Công văn 5588/BGDĐT-GDPT (2026)** — MOET official guideline instructing provinces on deploying the QĐ 2422 AI Content Framework across schools (Ch 7).
- **Công văn 8585/SGDĐT-GDPT (2026 - TP.HCM)** — HCMC municipal operational guideline for digital education, smart schools, and AI implementation (Ch 8).
- **Đánh giá quá trình (Formative Assessment)** — In-process evaluation of learner progress, effort, critical inquiry, and iterative reflection using rubrics and observations rather than high-stakes tests (Ch 6).
- **Deepfake** — Synthetic audiovisual media generated by deep neural networks engineered to realistically mimic real human individuals (Ch 4).
- **Digital Literacy (DL - Học vấn số hóa phổ thông)** — The fundamental branch of Informatics providing universal competence in navigating digital environments safely and effectively (Ch 1).
- **Độ chính xác, Chuẩn xác, Thu hồi, Điểm F1 (Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score)** — Quantitative classification evaluation metrics reflecting different performance tradeoffs across balanced and imbalanced datasets (Ch 5).
- **Đồng tâm xoắn ốc (Spiral Progression)** — The pedagogical principle where concepts are introduced simply at early grades and revisited with greater depth, abstraction, and rigor at higher grades (Ch 2).
- **Giáo dục AI không dùng máy tính (Unplugged AI)** — Kinesthetic, paper-based, or roleplaying instructional activities teaching computational logic without computer screens (Ch 6).
- **Hàm kích hoạt (Activation Function)** — Non-linear mathematical operators (e.g., ReLU, Sigmoid) enabling artificial neural networks to learn non-linear decision boundaries (Ch 5).
- **Hệ chuyên gia (Expert System)** — A rule-based symbolic AI system executing deterministic IF-THEN rules encoded by human subject matter experts (Ch 4).
- **Học có giám sát (Supervised Learning)** — Machine learning paradigm where an algorithm learns mappings between input features and human-provided target labels (Ch 5).
- **Học không giám sát (Unsupervised Learning)** — Machine learning paradigm discovering inherent structural groupings or patterns in unlabeled datasets (Ch 5).
- **Học máy (Machine Learning - ML)** — Algorithmic approaches that allow computers to discover statistical patterns from empirical data rather than through explicit conditional instructions (Ch 3, Ch 4).
- **Học tăng cường (Reinforcement Learning - RL)** — Machine learning paradigm where an autonomous agent optimizes behavioral policies via reward and punishment signals from an environment (Ch 5).
- **Information & Communication Technology (ICT - Công nghệ thông tin và truyền thông)** — The branch of Informatics emphasizing software application, multimedia productivity, and collaborative digital platforms (Ch 1).
- **Kế hoạch giáo dục nhà trường (School Education Plan)** — The autonomous operational curriculum formulated annually by each school to allocate subjects, experiential activities, and elective modules (Ch 7).
- **Kho học liệu số dùng chung (Open Digital Learning Repository)** — Shared institutional repositories containing accredited lesson plans, digital media, interactive simulations, and AI training datasets (Ch 8).
- **Lan truyền thuận & Lan truyền ngược (Forward & Backward Propagation)** — The foundational neural network training cycle of computing layer outputs forward and distributing loss gradients backward to update weights (Ch 5).
- **Makerspace (Không gian sáng chế / STEM Lab)** — Dedicated collaborative school workspaces equipped with digital fabrication tools, micro-controllers, and robotics hardware (Ch 8).
- **Mạng nơ-ron nhân tạo (Artificial Neural Network - ANN)** — Layered computational architectures composed of interconnected nodes inspired by biological brain structures (Ch 5).
- **Mô hình ngôn ngữ lớn (Large Language Model - LLM)** — Scaled foundation neural networks trained on massive multilingual textual corpora capable of contextual reasoning and text generation (Ch 4, Ch 5).
- **Năng lực Tin học (Informatics Competence)** — The 5 statutory competencies (NLa through NLe) defining digital capability under Thông tư 32/2018 (Ch 1).
- **Nguyên tắc không thu phí bổ sung (Zero-Fee Mandate)** — Statutory policy prohibiting schools from forcing families to purchase paid software subscriptions, premium SaaS tokens, or proprietary hardware (Ch 7).
- **Prompt Engineering (Kĩ thuật thiết kế câu lệnh nhắc)** — The systematic discipline of crafting structured context, persona, constraints, and examples to guide generative AI models toward accurate outputs (Ch 4).
- **Quyết định 2422/QĐ-BGDĐT (2026)** — The definitive legal decree issued by MOET establishing the National AI Content Framework for General Education (Ch 2).
- **Rò rỉ dữ liệu (Data Leakage)** — The critical machine learning error of allowing test set information to contaminate training preprocessing, creating artificially inflated accuracy (Ch 5).
- **Sinh hoạt chuyên môn theo nghiên cứu bài học (Lesson Study)** — Collaborative teacher development workflow based on collective lesson planning, classroom observation, and reflective post-lesson dialogue (Ch 7).
- **Tập huấn luyện & Tập kiểm thử (Training Set & Test Set)** — The mandatory partition of data into independent subsets to evaluate whether a model generalizes rather than memorizes (Ch 4).
- **Thiết bị thông minh (Smart Device)** — Everyday hardware appliances embedded with sensors, microprocessors, and software algorithms capable of autonomous or adaptive response (Ch 3).
- **Thiên vị dữ liệu (Algorithmic / Data Bias)** — Unfair distortions in AI model outputs caused by unrepresentative, incomplete, or culturally skewed training datasets (Ch 4, Ch 5).
- **Thị giác máy tính (Computer Vision - CV)** — AI subdiscipline enabling computational systems to process, segment, and understand digital images and video streams (Ch 4).
- **Thông tư 32/2018/TT-BGDĐT** — The landmark MOET circular promulgating the General Education Program 2018 (GDPT 2018) (Ch 1).
- **Tính nhân văn trong AI (Human-Centric AI)** — The foundational ethical philosophy mandating that AI serve human welfare, preserve human agency, and remain under human control (Ch 2).
- **Trợ lý giọng nói (Voice Assistant)** — Conversational software agents that interpret spoken commands and provide synthesized speech answers (Ch 3).
- **Trường học thông minh (Smart School)** — Comprehensive educational institutions integrating digital infrastructure, paperless governance, and smart spaces to optimize teaching and learning (Ch 8).
- **Xã hội hóa giáo dục (Educational Socialization / PPP)** — Transparent mobilization of external social, corporate, and philanthropic resources under strict academic and ethical governance (Ch 8).
- **Xử lý ngôn ngữ tự nhiên (Natural Language Processing - NLP)** — AI subdiscipline focused on computational analysis, translation, and generation of human text and speech (Ch 4).
- **Yêu cầu cần đạt (YCCĐ - Learning Outcomes)** — Concrete, observable, and measurable performance standards defined per grade level in MOET curriculum documents (Ch 1, Ch 2).
