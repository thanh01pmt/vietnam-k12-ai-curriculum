## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 1. The Human-Centric AI Education Philosophy (QĐ 2422 Part I)
- **Principle**: Artificial Intelligence is an engineered computational artifact created by humans, evaluated by humans, and strictly subservient to human welfare and autonomy.
- **Operational Rule**: Students must never view AI as sentient, infallible, or emotionally aware. Critical skepticism, source verification, and human ethical oversight are non-negotiable. The human user retains 100% legal, moral, and academic responsibility for AI-assisted outputs.

### 2. The 4 AI Curricular Knowledge Threads (4 Mạch Nội Dung)
All K-12 AI educational activities in Vietnam must map into one or more of these four continuous threads:
- **Thread A: Khái niệm & Ứng dụng AI (Concepts & Applications)** — Demystifying what AI is, comparing rule-based vs data-driven systems, identifying everyday smart devices, and understanding capability boundaries.
- **Thread B: Dữ liệu & Học máy (Data & Machine Learning)** — The data foundation: data collection, cleaning, representation, annotation, training/testing splits, overfitting, and algorithmic bias.
- **Thread C: Kĩ thuật & Hệ thống AI (Techniques & Systems)** — Computational mechanisms: computer vision (CV), natural language processing (NLP), decision trees, neural networks (ANN), and prompt engineering.
- **Thread D: Tác động Xã hội & Đạo đức AI (Societal Impact & Ethics)** — Digital safety, copyright, academic integrity, deepfakes, labor transformation, algorithmic fairness, and child privacy (Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP).

### 3. Spiral Progression Model (Mô hình Đồng tâm Xoắn ốc)
- **Primary Tier (Grades 1–5)**: Sensory exploration, distinguishing living beings from smart machines, observing sensors, simple visual classification with Teachable Machine, screen time regulation.
- **Lower Secondary Tier (Grades 6–9)**: Structured reasoning, rule-based vs machine learning, 5-step ML pipeline, CV and NLP concepts, CLEAR prompt engineering, identifying hallucinations and deepfakes.
- **Upper Secondary Tier (Grades 10–12)**: Architectural analysis, artificial neural networks (ANN), quantitative evaluation (Precision, Recall, F1), transfer learning, API integration, national and global AI governance.

### 4. Mandatory 12-Period Minimum Allocation (Công văn 5588 & 5208)
Starting in academic year 2026–2027, every K-12 student in Vietnam must receive a minimum of **12 periods per year** of AI education, integrated flexibly through:
1. *Informatics Integration (Môn Tin học)* — within regular Strands A, D, E, F.
2. *Interdisciplinary STEM Modules* — project-based units linking science and tech.
3. *Experiential Activities (HĐTN, HN)* — ethical debates and digital citizenship.
4. *Autonomous School Elective* — dedicated school timetable hours.

### 5. Statutory Zero-Fee & FOSS Mandate (Nguyên tắc Bình đẳng và Không thu phí)
- **Zero Financial Burden**: Absolute legal prohibition against requiring parents to purchase paid commercial software subscriptions, cloud token packages, or proprietary single-vendor hardware kits.
- **Open Access**: Instructional delivery must rely on free, open-source, client-side, or zero-install browser tools (e.g., Google Teachable Machine, Scratch, Python, micro:bit simulators).

### 6. Process-First, Zero-Exam Assessment Architecture (QĐ 2422 Part VI)
- **Formative Priority**: Assessment is process-driven (70% weighting on research logs, data hygiene, error analysis, and teamwork) using rubrics and portfolios.
- **No Separate Testing**: Schools are strictly forbidden from creating separate high-stakes written AI exams. AI marks integrate directly into regular Informatics/Technology grades.
