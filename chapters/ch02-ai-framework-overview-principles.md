# Chapter 2: National K-12 AI Curriculum Framework — Overview & Guiding Principles

## Core Idea
Promulgated under Quyết định 2422/QĐ-BGDĐT (2026), the National AI Education Content Framework for General Education provides the definitive normative roadmap for K-12 AI literacy in Vietnam. It grounds all AI learning in a human-centric philosophy, organizing competencies into four continuous knowledge threads across Grades 1 to 12.

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Human-Centric AI Education Philosophy (Triết lý AI vì con người & Con người làm chủ)**:
  - When to use: Designing any AI course, lesson, or evaluation metric to prevent technocentric bias or student dependency.
  - How:
    1. *Agency First*: Students must understand AI as an engineered computational artifact created by humans, evaluated by humans, and subservient to human flourishing.
    2. *Critical Scrutiny*: AI outputs must never be treated as authoritative truth; verification, skepticism, and human oversight are mandatory.
    3. *Ethical Responsibility*: The human user retains 100% legal, academic, and moral responsibility for actions and artifacts produced with AI assistance.
  - Why it works / failure mode: When students perceive AI as an autonomous, infallible intelligence, they surrender critical thinking and agency. Human-centricity anchors AI as an assistive lever.

- **The 4 AI Curricular Knowledge Threads (4 Mạch nội dung giáo dục AI)**:
  - When to use: Structuring balanced multi-year curriculum roadmaps and verifying learning outcome coverage.
  - How:
    - **Thread A: Khái niệm và Ứng dụng AI (AI Concepts & Applications)**: What AI is, how it differs from conventional software, everyday manifestations, smart devices, and capability boundaries.
    - **Thread B: Dữ liệu và Học máy (Data & Machine Learning)**: The lifeblood of modern AI—data collection, quality, representation, labeling, patterns, training, and algorithmic bias.
    - **Thread C: Kĩ thuật và Ứng dụng AI (AI Techniques & Algorithms)**: Computational mechanisms—rule-based logic, decision trees, computer vision, natural language processing, neural networks, and prompt engineering.
    - **Thread D: Tác động xã hội và Đạo đức AI (Societal Impact & Ethics)**: Copyright, academic integrity, privacy, deepfakes, labor displacement, digital divide, and safety governance.
  - Why it works / failure mode: Neglecting Thread B leads to treating AI as magic; neglecting Thread D creates technically proficient but reckless or unethical users.

- **Spiral Progression Model (Mô hình phát triển đồng tâm xoắn ốc)**:
  - When to use: Mapping a single concept across school tiers (Primary $\rightarrow$ Lower Secondary $\rightarrow$ Upper Secondary).
  - How:
    - *Primary (Lớp 1–5)*: Intuitive observation, recognition, physical/visual interaction, distinguish real vs virtual, simple classification.
    - *Lower Secondary (Lớp 6–9)*: Mechanistic explanation, structured data pipelines, rule-based vs learning-based systems, recognizing bias, prompt refinement.
    - *Upper Secondary (Lớp 10–12)*: Architectural understanding, neural network principles, system prototyping, career pathways, policy and ethics evaluation.

## Key Concepts
- **Quyết định 2422/QĐ-BGDĐT**: The official 2026 Ministry of Education and Training decree establishing the benchmark K-12 AI Content Framework.
- **Trí tuệ nhân tạo (Artificial Intelligence - AI)**: Computer systems designed to simulate human cognitive functions, such as learning, reasoning, pattern recognition, and problem solving.
- **Tính nhân văn trong AI (Human-Centered AI)**: The educational principle that human well-being, dignity, rights, and autonomy must guide the design, deployment, and utilization of AI.
- **Tính mở và linh hoạt (Open and Dynamic Architecture)**: The structural property of the 2422 framework permitting schools and educators to update tools and examples as technology rapidly evolves.
- **Yêu cầu cần đạt (Learning Outcomes / YCCĐ)**: Measurable, observable behavioral benchmarks prescribed by MOET for every grade level and thread.
- **Đồng tâm phát triển (Concentric / Spiral Development)**: The pedagogical technique of revisiting foundational topics at increasing depths as cognitive maturity increases.

## Mental Models
- **"AI is a mirror of data, not a mind of its own"**: AI models reflect the data fed to them; they possess neither consciousness, intent, nor moral compass.
- **"The 4 Threads are four legs of a table"**: If a curriculum drops any one thread (e.g., teaching only coding algorithms without ethics or data concepts), the student's understanding collapses into distortion.

## Anti-patterns
- **The "Black Box Miracle" Pattern**: Demonstrating generative AI tools (chatbots, image generators) purely as spectacle without revealing the underlying data-driven pattern prediction mechanisms.
- **Preach-and-Forget Ethics**: Confining ethics to a dry 15-minute lecture on copyright rather than having students audit datasets for bias or detect deepfakes hands-on.
- **Grade Inflation of Rigor**: Forcing high school matrix calculus or gradient descent proofs onto lower secondary students before they grasp algorithmic flowcharts.

## Reference Tables

### 4 Threads Progression Across Educational Stages

| Thread | Primary School (Lớp 1–5) | Lower Secondary (Lớp 6–9) | Upper Secondary (Lớp 10–12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thread A: Concepts & Apps** | Recognizing smart devices; voice assistants; toys with sensors; AI vs human difference | Rule-based vs data-driven AI; limitations of AI; expert systems vs ML | System architecture; specialized vs general AI; AI frontier domains |
| **Thread B: Data & ML** | Recognizing data types (images, sounds); sorting and grouping items | Data collection, cleaning, labeling; training vs testing data; data bias | Feature engineering; training data distribution; data leakage & fairness |
| **Thread C: Techniques & Systems** | Interactive visual tools; block-based classification (Teachable Machine) | Computer vision & NLP pipelines; decision trees; structured prompt craft | Neural networks (ANN); perceptron; generative models; API integration |
| **Thread D: Ethics & Impact** | Screen time balance; digital privacy; never sharing passwords; AI has no feelings | Copyright, plagiarism, deepfake identification, responsible social media sharing | Algorithmic bias mitigation; intellectual property; economic & labor impacts |

### Alignment of AI Threads with GDPT 2018 Core Competencies

| Competency | Curricular AI Integration Pathway |
|---|---|
| **Tự chủ và tự học** | Using AI tutors and conversational tools for inquiry, self-assessment, and personalized review while maintaining intellectual autonomy. |
| **Giao tiếp và hợp tác** | Co-creating multimedia artifacts with AI agents; peer-reviewing AI-generated group projects; collaborative prompt engineering. |
| **Giải quyết vấn đề & Sáng tạo** | Formulating engineering prompts; decomposing complex civic or school problems into solvable data workflows. |
| **Trung thực (Integrity)** | Explicitly citing AI assistance; distinguishing personal original thinking from synthetic text; refusing academic ghostwriting. |
| **Trách nhiệm (Responsibility)** | Safeguarding personal and peer data; auditing models for harmful bias; respecting intellectual property laws. |

## Worked Example

### Curriculum Alignment Audit using QĐ 2422 Criteria
A school plans a 4-lesson STEM module titled "Smart Plant Watering System":

1. **Step 1: Map Proposed Module to the 4 Threads**:
   - *Thread A*: Explaining how camera-based leaf disease detection assists agriculture (Concepts & Apps).
   - *Thread B*: Taking 100 photos of healthy leaves and 100 photos of dry/diseased leaves to build a dataset (Data & ML).
   - *Thread C*: Training a lightweight visual classifier and triggering a servo pump via micro:bit (Techniques & Systems).
   - *Thread D*: Discussing what happens if the training dataset only contains one plant species in ideal lighting (Ethics & Bias).
2. **Step 2: Spiral Progression Check**:
   - Target Grade: Grade 8 (Lower Secondary).
   - Verifying against QD 2422 Grade 8 YCCĐ: "Nhận biết được vai trò của dữ liệu huấn luyện đối với chất lượng mô hình học máy." $\rightarrow$ **MATCHED**.
3. **Step 3: Human-Centricity Verification**:
   - Does the student retain control? Yes: The human gardener oversees the system, verifies leaf classification confidence thresholds, and can override the pump.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Human agency is non-negotiable**: AI is an assistive tool engineered by humans; students must never view AI as sentient or unquestionable.
2. **The 4 Threads must remain balanced**: Concepts (A), Data (B), Techniques (C), and Ethics (D) are inextricably bound across every grade level.
3. **Spiral progression matches cognitive readiness**: From sensory-based observation in Primary, to structured reasoning in Lower Secondary, to architectural analysis in Upper Secondary.
4. **Evaluation focuses on competency manifestation**: Success is measured by critical thinking, problem-solving, and ethical stewardship.
